## What it says up front

The introduction states the programme's goal as a **thermal breeder**:
- fuelled with ²³³UF₄ dissolved in a LiF–BeF₂ salt, though it could start up
  on U-235 or plutonium;
- with ThF₄ as the fertile material, in the same salt or in a separate
  blanket salt.

It repeats the two-fluid to one-fluid reasoning that
[ORNL-4449](ORNL-4449.md#why-the-breeder-became-one-fluid) gives.

**Open question for this report:** its period covers the U-233 loading. The
date the MSRE first went critical on U-233 should be stated here and would
settle the "October 1969" misprint in ORNL-4449. Not yet looked up.
