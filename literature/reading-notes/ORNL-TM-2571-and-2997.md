**Read how far (2026-11-17):** title pages only, plus the start of TM-2997's
abstract. Nothing else.

- **TM-2997's abstract:** tests were performed on the MSRE to determine the
  system's time response to step changes in reactivity, and the neutron
  flux-to-reactivity frequency response. The extracted text breaks off there.
- **Why these matter:** they are the U-233 counterparts of TM-1070 (theory)
  and TM-1647 (experiment). The backend's MSRE T-1 comparison and the
  simulator's Frequency tab already use those two for U-235. A U-233 version
  of the simulator would be judged against these.
- **For a LFTR model:** U-233's delayed-neutron fraction is smaller than
  U-235's (general knowledge, not taken from these reports; check TM-2571
  for the value it used). So these are the measured plant dynamics nearest to a
  thorium-cycle molten salt reactor that exist.
